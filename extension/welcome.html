What lives inside the toolbar menu

More than the floating popup you may have already seen on a webpage.

Epoch → Date

Paste any 10- or 13-digit timestamp. See the human date in every timezone you care about.

Date → Epoch

Pick a date, time, and timezone. Or paste an ISO 8601 string — get the epoch back instantly.

Relative time

"3 days, 2 hours from now" → epoch. Or the other way. Skip the mental math.

Conversion history

Your last 10 conversions are kept locally — pull them back any time, or clear with one click.

Multi-timezone

Add any timezones you work with — every result shows them side-by-side. No tab-juggling.

Floating popup

Highlight any epoch on a webpage — it pops up inline. The shortcut you may have already discovered.

Offline-first. Yours alone.

Every conversion runs locally in your browser. No server calls, no logging of timestamps. Anonymous click counts can be turned off in Settings.
